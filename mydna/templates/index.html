What happens to your file

  • It is read directly from the request and parsed in memory. It is never written to disk.
  • Only the variant identifiers are kept. Your genotype calls are discarded immediately.
  • Nothing leaves this machine. The lookup runs against the local index, and this page loads no third-party resources.
  • Your report is held in memory so you can download the PDF, then discarded when you clear it or restart the app.

This is not a medical test. A ClinVar entry means the archive holds a submitted interpretation for a variant — not that you have, or will develop, any condition. Consumer genotyping arrays are prone to false positives at rare variants in particular. Nothing here should change a medical decision without confirmation from a clinician or genetic counsellor.
